Authentic. Fresh. Reliable.

Founded by Pratham Kotecha, Nexaus Traders Pvt. Ltd. brings the authentic taste of India to the world. Specializing in premium spices and agricultural products, we actively export to key global markets including the UK, Saudi Arabia, and Africa.

Built on foundational values of purity, honesty, and excellence, we don't just complete transactions; we forge long-term partnerships. Every grain and spice is meticulously sourced and processed to retain its natural aroma, brilliant color, and authentic flavor profile.

Our Infrastructure

Inside the Factory

State-of-the-art heavy-duty pulverizers and vibratory sifters ensure every spice we process meets rigorous global purity standards.

Spice Grinding & Milling

Our main production floor featuring heavy-duty pulverizers that break down raw spices into fine powder while retaining essential oils.

Vibratory Sifting

Multi-level sifters separating fine powder to ensure perfectly uniform consistency.

Continuous Feeding

Automated hoppers feeding raw materials smoothly into the high-capacity milling units.

Bulk Collection

Freshly ground spices are directly collected into bulk sacks right off the processing line.
